How The Campus System Works

Structured, verified, and secure recovery loop for the campus community

1
📋

1. Report & Tag

Submit item details, specify exact campus block (Mechanical, Civil, IT, New IT, Admin, MBA, PU College), upload photos, and flag if urgent.

2
🤖

2. Search & Smart Match

Instant category filters and our Smart Match engine find possible lost-found pairings automatically across campus blocks.

3
🔒

3. Private Proof Verification

Claimants submit non-public identifying details (stickers, serial numbers, wallpaper, contents) to verify ownership safely.

4
🖨️

4. Verified Handover Slip

Collect at designated campus drop points and generate a signed handover receipt for formal custody records.

📜 Campus Handover Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

  1. Finder: Report the item on this portal and hand it over to the nearest designated center within 4 hours.
  2. Verification: The claimant must describe non-public details (wallpaper, stickers, bill or internal contents).
  3. Collection: Present a valid ID card when picking up the item at the handover desk.
  4. Receipt: An automated digital Handover Receipt is generated on this portal for official record.
