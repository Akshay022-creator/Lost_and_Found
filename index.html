📜 Campus Handover Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

  1. Finder: When you find an item, report it online and hand it over to the nearest designated center within 4 hours.
  2. Verification: The claimant must describe non-public details (wallpaper, stickers, bill or internal contents).
  3. Collection: Present your valid College ID Card (USN) when picking up the item.
  4. Receipt: An automated digital Handover Receipt is generated on this portal for official record.
